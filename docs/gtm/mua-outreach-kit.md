## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Is it free?" | Yes to join and list. Commission applies only on completed platform bookings — no upfront cost, no monthly fee. |
| "I already have clients." | This adds *new* clients who don't already have your contact, plus upfront payment protects you from chasing money. |
| "How do I get paid?" | Client pays through Billplz; your payout is recorded when the balance is paid and becomes settleable ~1 day after the event date. |

⚠️ **Accuracy notes (fix before sending):**

1. **Commission %:** this kit previously said **12%**; the platform default is
   **10%** (`commission_rate_bps` in Admin → Settings). Pick ONE number and make
   the kit + settings match before first contact.
2. **"Escrow / released after completion":** technically the client pays a small
   deposit to confirm and the balance before the event; the payout is recorded
   then and settles after the event date. Don't promise post-completion escrow
   you don't operate.

## Onboarding checklist (per artist)

1. Create their catalog profile: **Admin → Artists → "+ Add Artist"**
   (name, state, area, price-from; tagline optional)
2. Have them register at `/register` with role **Artist** and verify email
3. They claim their catalog profile at `/onboarding` → paste profile id
4. Add services/pricing via Admin → Artists → edit (or they self-edit)
5. Confirm a test booking round-trip in dev/staging
6. Log them in `outreach-tracker.csv`
