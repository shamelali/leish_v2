## 1. The Service

1.1 Leish! operates an online marketplace (`[https://leish.my]`) that connects
beauty professionals with clients seeking makeup and beauty services in
Malaysia. Leish! provides listing, booking, quotation, messaging, payment
collection and payout tooling.

1.2 **Marketplace role.** Leish! is not a party to the service contract between
a Client and a Provider for any booked beauty service. The Provider performs
all beauty services as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement
creates employment, agency, partnership or joint venture between the Parties.

## 2. Provider Obligations

2.1 Maintain accurate profile information (identity, qualifications, portfolio,
pricing, service area).

2.2 Respond to booking requests within `[24 HOURS]`; issue quotations through
the Platform within the quotation validity window shown to the Client.

2.3 Perform booked services at the agreed date, time and location, in a
professional manner consistent with prevailing industry standards and all
applicable Malaysian laws and regulations, including health and hygiene
requirements.

2.4 Only accept bookings for dates/times you can honour. Repeated no-shows are
a material breach (see Clause 7).

2.5 Keep all Client personal data confidential per the NDA referenced in
Clause 12 and the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 ("PDPA").

## 3. Bookings, Quotations & Payments

3.1 **Flow.** A Client submits a booking request → you accept or decline → you
issue a Quotation through the Platform → the Client pays a **Booking Deposit**
to confirm → the Client pays the remaining **Balance** before the event.

3.2 **Deposit.** The deposit amount is set by Leish! from time to time
(currently `RM [50]`, configurable in Platform Settings) and is collected by
Leish! on your behalf. A confirmed booking reserves the slot exclusively.

3.3 **Quotations.** Quotations must reflect the final price actually agreed.
Quotation validity is `[24 HOURS]` unless extended by the Platform.

3.4 **Balance.** Unless otherwise agreed on-Platform, the Balance is due no
later than `[3 DAYS]` before the event date. Leish! may send automated
reminders to the Client.

3.5 **Payment methods.** All Client payments must be processed through the
Platform's payment gateway(s). Accepting cash or off-platform payment for a
Platform-introduced booking is prohibited and constitutes material breach.

## 4. Commissions & Payouts

4.1 **Commission.** Leish! earns a commission on each completed booking,
currently `[10]%` of the Quotation total (configurable). Bookings whose total
is below `RM [100]` may be commission-free at Leish!'s discretion. The Client
always pays exactly the Quoted price; the commission is deducted from your
payout.

4.2 **Payout.** After each fully-paid booking, the Platform records a payout:

    net payout = quotation total − commission − deposit held

Payouts become settleable `[1 DAY]` after the event date and are transferred
to your designated bank account within `[7 BUSINESS DAYS]` of becoming
settleable, subject to Clause 6 (cancellations/refunds).

4.3 **Statements.** You can view bookings, payouts and their status
(pending/settled) in your dashboard at any time. Report discrepancies within
`[14 DAYS]`.

4.4 **Fees & taxes.** You are responsible for your own income tax, SST (if
registered) and any bank charges. Leish! will provide annual earning summaries
on request.

## 5. Cancellation, Refunds & Disputes

5.1 **Client cancellation more than `[3 DAYS]` before the event:** the Balance
(if paid) is refunded in full; the Deposit may be retained per the refund
policy published on the Platform `[OR: refunded less a RM X handling fee]`.

5.2 **Client cancellation within `[3 DAYS]` of the event:** amounts paid are
non-refundable, and the Provider receives the payout for work already
secured `[OR: split % — CHOOSE POLICY]`.

5.3 **Provider cancellation:** cancelling a confirmed booking is a serious
breach. First occurrence: warning + full Client refund from your payout.
Repeated occurrences: suspension or termination per Clause 8, plus liability
for Client-consequential costs reasonably incurred.

5.4 **No-show disputes.** Leish! may mediate using on-platform records
(messages, timestamps, payment status) as the primary evidence source.

5.5 Chargebacks initiated against Leish! that are attributable to Provider
conduct may be debited from the Provider's pending or future payouts.

## 6. Ratings, Reviews & Conduct

6.1 Clients may leave reviews for completed bookings. Reviews may not be
manipulated (incentivised, filtered, or traded). Disputes over defamatory
reviews may be raised via `[SUPPORT EMAIL]`.

6.2 Prohibited conduct includes harassment, discrimination, misrepresentation
of qualifications or products, off-platform solicitation of Platform
introductions, and use of the Platform to sell unapproved retail goods.
