## 1. Confidential Information

1.1 "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by
one Party ("Discloser") to the other ("Recipient"), in any form, that is
marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential given
its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. It includes:

- business plans, pricing, commission and fee structures;
- platform metrics, user statistics and financial data;
- software, source code, system architecture and security practices;
- supplier, client, artist and studio lists;
- personal data of platform users;
- marketing strategies and unpublished materials.

1.2 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement;
(b) was lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure;
(c) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's information;
(d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction; or
(e) is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, court order or a competent
authority — provided the Recipient gives the Discloser prompt written notice
(where lawful) so it may seek protective treatment, and discloses only the
minimum required.

## 2. Obligations

2.1 The Recipient shall:

(a) keep the Discloser's Confidential Information strictly confidential;
(b) use it solely for the Purpose and for no other benefit;
(c) limit access to employees, contractors and advisers who need it for the
Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as strict as
this Agreement (the Recipient remains liable for their acts);
(d) apply at least the same degree of care it applies to its own confidential
information, and no less than reasonable care;
(e) promptly notify the Discloser of any unauthorised use or disclosure.

2.2 **Return or destruction.** On the Purpose ending, or on the Discloser's
written request, the Recipient shall promptly return or securely destroy all
Confidential Information (including copies), except one archival copy required
by law or bona fide backup systems, which remains bound by this Agreement.

## 3. Personal Data

3.1 Where Confidential Information includes personal data protected under the
Malaysian **Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA)**, the Recipient shall
process it only on the Discloser's documented instructions, implement
appropriate technical and organisational security measures, and assist the
Discloser with data-subject requests. This clause supplements, and does not
replace, any separate data-processing agreement.

## 4. No Licence; No Warranty

4.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser.
No licence, assignment or other rights in intellectual property are granted
except the limited right to use it for the Purpose.

4.2 All Confidential Information is provided "as is". Neither Party warrants
its accuracy or completeness.

## 5. Term & Duration

5.1 This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues for
`[2 YEARS]`, unless ended earlier by either Party on `[30 DAYS']` written
notice.

5.2 **Survival.** The confidentiality obligations survive termination for
`[3 YEARS]` from the date of each disclosure — and indefinitely for trade
secrets, source code, and personal data.

## 6. Remedies

6.1 The Parties acknowledge that breach may cause irreparable harm for which
damages alone are inadequate. The Discloser may seek injunctive relief in
addition to all other remedies, including recovery of losses directly caused
by the breach.

## 7. General

7.1 **Governing law:** Malaysia. Exclusive jurisdiction: courts of
`[KUALA LUMPUR]`.
7.2 This Agreement is the entire agreement on confidentiality regarding the
Purpose and supersedes prior understandings. Amendments must be in writing and
signed by both Parties.
7.3 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent.
7.4 Failure to enforce any provision is not a waiver of it.
