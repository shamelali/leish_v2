Straight talk

Who this works for — and who it doesn't

Outbound is not magic and it is not for everybody. We would rather turn down a pilot than take money for a campaign that cannot work in your market.

Good fit
  • B2B services or products with a named buyer (safety, logistics, manufacturing, software, professional services)
  • Deal values from roughly RM 10,000 upward, where one meeting pays for the month
  • Sales cycles of weeks or months — enough time for a sequence to work
  • Someone on your side who can actually attend the meetings we book
  • A website, catalogue or product page we can research from
Not a fit
  • B2C impulse purchases — ads and content do that job far better
  • Low-ticket products where a meeting costs more than the margin
  • Anyone expecting booked revenue in week one instead of conversations
  • Sending to a bought, rented or scraped list — we decline those campaigns outright
  • Markets where your buyer is not reachable by email or WhatsApp at all

Questions buyers actually ask

Outbound, answered plainly

How fast will I see results?

First replies typically land in week two, once volume has ramped and the copy has been tested on a real audience. Meetings usually start appearing in weeks three and four. Anyone promising signed contracts inside seven days is either lying or spamming — and spam does not survive Malaysian B2B relationships.

Do I need to give you my email domain?

No. By default we send from warmed subdomains on our own infrastructure, which means your primary domain's reputation and your everyday email keep working untouched. If you would rather send from your own domain, we configure SPF, DKIM and DMARC, ramp the volume and monitor it — and we will tell you upfront if we think your domain is too new or too damaged for it.

Will the emails sound like a robot wrote them?

That is the failure mode we design against. Every email references something verifiable about that specific business, keeps one clear ask, and avoids the inflated claims that make Malaysian buyers delete instantly. A human strategist reviews the first batches before they send, and you approve the templates before volume goes up.

What if someone complains about being contacted?

We stop immediately, add them to the central suppression list so no campaign touches them again, and can show you exactly where their business contact details came from. Every send is logged with its source precisely so this conversation is short and factual. That is also why we refuse bought lists — with purchased data you cannot answer the question honestly.

Can the engine use WhatsApp, or just email?

Both, where appropriate. WhatsApp is often the channel that closes the loop in Malaysia, so we use the WhatsApp Business API for opted-in conversations and follow-ups on numbers that are not on the Do Not Call registry. Cold WhatsApp blasting is exactly the behaviour that gets a number reported — so we do not do it.

What happens to the research if I stop?

It is yours. You keep the segment map, ICP definition, sequences, the verified contact data we compiled for you, and the performance history — handed over in a format you can use. Outbound should be a transferable asset, not a subscription you rent forever.
