Straight answers

Common questions

No sales theatre. If you would rather see proof than prose, ask us for a live walkthrough of a shipped system in your industry.

What do I need to get started with the outbound engine?

Your website or a short description of what you sell, plus one call to agree the segments. No SDRs to hire, no email infrastructure to set up, no domain to warm yourself — we handle the sending stack, the research and the copy. If you have no website, the free audit still works from your business name and Google Business Profile.

How much does it cost?

Two parts: the retainer that covers strategy, research, copy and reporting (add-on from RM 1,500/month), and pay-as-you-go email credits (from roughly RM 0.15 per verified business contact, volume dependent). Retainers are month-to-month and include a free initial audit; there is no subscription lock-in on the send credits.

Whose mailboxes send the emails? Is my domain at risk?

By default we send from a warmed subdomain under our own infrastructure, so your primary domain's reputation is untouched. If you prefer to send from your own domain, we configure SPF, DKIM and DMARC properly, ramp the volume deliberately and monitor deliverability from day one — and we will tell you honestly if your domain is not ready.

Is cold outreach even allowed under PDPA in Malaysia?

PDPA 2010 governs personal data, so we keep campaigns inside narrow, defensible boundaries: business-to-business contact details that are published for business purposes, no sensitive personal data, a working opt-out on every send, a centrally maintained suppression list, and no outreach to numbers on the Do Not Call registry.

Our PDPA team reviews each campaign's data sources and copy before it sends, and we document what we did — useful if a prospect ever asks. We are engineers with a compliance practice, not your lawyers; for formal legal advice we will work alongside your counsel.

How much control do I have over what goes out?

You approve the segments and the sequence copy before anything sends. Budget caps are agreed in writing. You can pause the engine at any time from the dashboard, and every send is logged with the source of the contact so the audit trail exists if you need it.

What happens when a prospect replies?

The agent answers routine questions, handles the natural objections, and offers specific slots from your calendar — in BM or English. Anything commercial, legal or unusual is escalated to your team with the full thread attached, and every booked meeting lands in your CRM with the context of how it was won.

Can I bring my own leads?

Yes. Import an existing CSV or CRM export, and the engine enriches, verifies and sequences them instead of starting from zero. If your list was bought or scraped, we will not send to it — that is where Malaysian SMEs get burned, and it is the one thing we refuse.

What are the backup and security standards on Foundation?

Recovery targets are agreed at onboarding — typically under 4 hours RTO with a 4-hourly RPO, tighter for critical systems and scoped up front — and we run monthly restore tests so backups are proven recoverable, not assumed. Monitoring is 24/7 with alerts acknowledged within 15 minutes, EDR runs on managed endpoints, and the incident response plan is documented.

Who owns the custom AI systems you build for us?

You do. Custom code and configuration are handed over with documentation and repository access, so you are never held hostage by your vendor. Third-party platform subscriptions stay in your name. This is one of the reasons contracts here are month-to-month rather than three-year commitments.

You are a young company — who actually supports us?

You get a named engineer who knows your environment, backed by 24/7 automated monitoring and an after-hours on-call runbook — not a rotating stranger on every ticket. SSM details and client references are shared on request, because as a 2023-founded company we expect you to do your homework.
